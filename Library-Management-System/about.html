About the Library of Vietnam National University

The Library of Vietnam National University is one of the largest and most modern libraries in the region, serving the academic and research needs of students, faculty members, and researchers. With the aim of supporting learning, scientific research, and cultural development, the library offers a wide range of resources including books, journals, newspapers, and electronic materials in various fields of study.

Library Services

  • Book Lending: The library offers a free book lending service for students and faculty members, with a collection of books from a wide variety of subjects ranging from basic sciences to social sciences and humanities.
  • Reading Newspapers and Journals: The library provides access to a variety of newspapers and journals, offering up-to-date information and research materials.
  • Electronic Resources: The library offers electronic resources such as e-books and research databases, allowing users to access materials online easily.
  • Study Rooms and Group Workspaces: The library provides quiet study rooms and group workspaces equipped with necessary tools, creating a conducive environment for learning and research.

Electronic Library System

The university’s electronic library system provides online services that allow users to search for books, articles, theses, and other research materials remotely. Users can easily search and borrow materials without having to visit the library in person. The electronic library system also provides a platform for storing digital documents for students, faculty, and researchers.

Student Support Activities

In addition to providing academic resources, the library also serves as a space that supports the overall development of students. The library organizes workshops and training sessions on research skills, information retrieval, and other extracurricular activities to enhance students' academic skills.
